    Points count matched 80×24 screens, one per recorded step where the port's render matches C exactly. Screen is points as a share of the maximum. PRNG is the share of matched random-number calls, weighted across pools by each pool's maximum points, as on the leaderboard. Sessions counts sessions matched end to end. The pool switch splits these into the 44 public sessions and the 44 held-out ones.

    Anim counts matched animation frames. Startup and ms per move are the two terms of the judge's linear speed fit. Playable is the judge's browser check. None of these four count toward the ranking.

    The chart starts at the judge's scoring-rule change on May 5, 2026, 23:55 UTC, as the leaderboard's own sparkline does; the two earlier schemes produced point totals that are not comparable. Runs where the judge could not score a fork carry that fork's last good numbers.
