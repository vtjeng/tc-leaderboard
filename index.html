    Click a column header to sort; select a row to highlight that team in the chart.

    • Points count matched 80×24 screens: one point per recorded step where the port's render matches C exactly. The leaderboard's Screen percentage is points as a share of the maximum shown under the column header; hover a Points cell to see it.
    • PRNG is the share of matched random-number calls. Combined weights each pool by its maximum points, as on the leaderboard.
    • Sessions counts sessions matched end to end.
    • Pool applies to the whole page. Combined sums the 44 public and 44 held-out sessions, as the leaderboard ranks them; Public or Held-out shows just that pool in the chart and table.
    • Anim counts matched animation frames.
    • Startup and Per move are the two terms of the judge's linear speed fit, startup_ms + per_move_ms × moves, which is not split by pool. The table's Speed column shows both.
    • Playable is the judge's browser check: the build loads in headless Chromium without errors and stays under the judge's time limit per move.
    • The official leaderboard ranks teams by points; Anim, Startup, Per move and Playable are shown there for information only. This page's default order matches it (combined points), but you can sort the table by any column.
    • The chart starts at the judge's scoring-rule change on May 5, 2026, 23:55 UTC, as the leaderboard's own sparkline does. The two earlier scoring schemes produced point totals that are not comparable.
    • When the judge cannot score a fork, that run carries the fork's last good numbers.
